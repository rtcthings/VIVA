Join the VIVA Community

VIVA celebrates visionary nonprofits that are making Las Vegas stronger, more resilient, and more connected. By registering your nonprofit, you'll have the opportunity to share your mission, connect with local funders, and be considered for our next round of community granting.

What We're Looking For

We're seeking nonprofits that are:

✦
Innovative

bringing fresh ideas to local challenges.

✦
Community-Focused

rooted in serving and uplifting Las Vegas.

✦
Impact-Driven

focused on measurable outcomes for the community.
