Become a VIVA Funder

At VIVA, we believe in the power of collective giving. By becoming a funder, you're not only investing in bold ideas, you're helping shape the future of Las Vegas. Together, we can provide the resources nonprofits need to bring visionary solutions to life.

Why Fund with VIVA?

✦
Transparent Impact:

100% of your contribution goes directly to local nonprofits.

✦
Community-Driven:

✦
Scalable Change:

Every dollar raised is pooled to maximize reach and effectiveness.
